# Islamic Content Guidelines

Applies to: Quran & Tafsir · Hadith & Sunnah · Prophetic stories · Sahabah & Islamic
history · Islamic education. **Accuracy is never sacrificed for drama.**

## 1. Quran

- Verify the **exact Arabic text**.
- Verify Surah name and Ayah number.
- Use reliable, recognized translations (Bengali and/or English as the project needs).
- Consult recognized Tafsir (e.g. Ibn Kathir, Al-Jalalayn, Tafhim-ul-Quran) for
  explanation; cite which tafsir a claim comes from in `RESEARCH.md`.

## 2. Hadith

- Verify collection (e.g. Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, etc.).
- Verify book/hadith number and narrator.
- Verify authenticity grading (sahih / hasan / da'if) per reliable scholarship and state
  it honestly.
- Explain the context.

## 3. Prophetic stories

- Priority sources: Quran and authentic Hadith.
- **Never invent dialogue.**
- **Never depict Prophets' faces** — use respectful symbolic visualization (light,
  silhouette, environment, objects, typography).
- Extend appropriate care to other revered figures as customary.
- Settings must be historically and geographically appropriate (no modern Bangladeshi
  rural scenery inserted without reason).

## 4. Religious audio

- Follow the audio approach selected at planning (state it in the production plan).
- Never present fabricated sounds as authentic religious events.

## 5. Research honesty (all categories)

- Reliable primary and secondary sources; cross-check important claims.
- Clearly distinguish established fact from uncertainty in `RESEARCH.md` and, where
  relevant, in the narration itself.
