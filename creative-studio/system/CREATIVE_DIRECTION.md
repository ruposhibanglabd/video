# Creative Direction

## 1. Primary creative identity

Every frame must read as: **PREMIUM · MODERN · CINEMATIC · NATURAL · ELEGANT ·
EDUCATIONAL · VISUALLY RICH · PROFESSIONALLY ART-DIRECTED.**

The benchmark is a high-end international animation & motion design studio.

**Never acceptable:** generic AI slideshows · cheap social templates · random AI images ·
basic text over stock footage · overly colorful childish animation · unprofessional
editing · repetitive visual sequences.

**Scene doctrine:** every scene has a deliberately designed composition; every frame
serves the story; every visual element has a reason to exist.

## 2. Bangladeshi visual identity & natural environment

When — and only when — the topic supports it, bring an authentic, sophisticated
Bangladeshi rural atmosphere:

- Green villages, rural roads, rivers & canals, traditional boats, paddy fields,
  village ponds, bamboo groves, coconut trees, rain-soaked greenery, rural houses,
  morning mist, golden afternoon light, river sunsets, seasonal landscapes, traditional
  architecture, authentic agricultural scenery.

**Treatment:** beautiful natural light, rich but realistic greens, warm golden sunlight,
soft atmospheric haze, natural color separation, refined cinematic contrast, detailed
environmental composition, smooth camera movement.

**Restraint rules:**
- Do **not** force rural Bangladesh into unrelated subjects (global science/history gets
  the environment it actually needs).
- Quranic / Islamic historical stories use **historically and geographically appropriate
  settings**, not modern Bangladeshi scenery inserted without reason.
- Bangladeshi identity enriches relevant scenes; it never distorts accuracy.

## 3. Cinematic scene production

Each scene is designed individually with 16 defined elements (full card in
[`../production/SCENE_PLANNING_TEMPLATE.md`](../production/SCENE_PLANNING_TEMPLATE.md)):
objective, narration, background, main subject, foreground, camera position, camera
movement, lighting, color grade, typography, motion graphics, animation, SFX, ambience,
transition, start/end timestamps.

Choose per scene the best of: realistic 3D animation · premium 2D animation ·
documentary footage · cinematic AI-generated visuals · scientific visualization ·
motion graphics · macro cinematography · historical illustration · typography-led
sequences.

**Never reuse one image repeatedly to fill time.**

## 4. Story doctrine

Strong opening hook → accurate, engaging narration → meaningful conclusion.
Research adapts to the category (A–L, see MASTER_INSTRUCTIONS §7). Accuracy is never
sacrificed for drama.
