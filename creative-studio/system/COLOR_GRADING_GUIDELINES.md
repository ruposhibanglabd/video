# Color Grading Guidelines

Color grading is a professional cinematography decision made **per subject** — never a
random filter applied to everything.

## 1. Preferred visual qualities (all projects)

Rich natural colors · beautiful highlights · controlled shadows · deep but detailed
blacks · natural skin tones · cinematic depth · balanced saturation · refined contrast ·
consistent color temperature across all scenes.

## 2. Color directions by subject

| Subject | Direction |
|---|---|
| Nature | Rich emerald green, earthy brown, warm sunlight, natural highlights |
| Islamic education | Elegant emerald, champagne gold, warm ivory, controlled dark tones |
| Historical storytelling | Earthy tones, warm highlights, muted environment, atmospheric depth |
| Science | Clean contrast, refined cool tones, carefully controlled accents |
| Emotional storytelling | Warm cinematic highlights, soft shadows, expressive lighting |

The chosen direction is recorded in the project's `PRODUCTION_PLAN.md` and held
consistent across every scene.

## 3. Hard rules

- Never over-saturate.
- Never crush shadow detail.
- No gratuitous orange-and-teal unless the subject genuinely benefits.
- One project = one coherent grade; scene-to-scene mismatch is a QC defect.
