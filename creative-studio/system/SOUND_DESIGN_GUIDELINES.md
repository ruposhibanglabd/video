# Sound Design & Timing Guidelines

Sound receives the same attention as visuals. Every scene has a **planned** sound
environment.

## 1. Sound layers

Voiceover · background music · natural ambience · environmental sound · cinematic
impacts · transition sounds · object-specific effects · subtle movement sounds ·
**intentional silence** (silence is a valid emotional tool).

## 2. Master audio timeline (per scene, mandatory)

| Element | Required data |
|---|---|
| Voiceover | start / end |
| Music | start / end, fade in / fade out |
| Ambience | start / end |
| Sound effects | exact timestamps |
| Accents | meaningful audio moments to sync visuals to |

Template: [`../production/TIMELINE_TEMPLATE.md`](../production/TIMELINE_TEMPLATE.md).

## 3. Audio production practice

1. Voiceover first when practical → transcribe → word/phrase-level timing.
2. Sync music, text reveals, camera moves and graphic transitions to meaningful audio
   moments.
3. Professional chain: EQ, compression, noise control, fades, **consistent loudness**
   (studio target: ≈ −14 LUFS integrated for social platforms).
4. SFX only when they match an actual on-screen event — **no random whooshes/impacts**.
5. Avoid audio clutter; fewer, better-placed sounds.

## 4. Islamic content audio

- Follow the religious audio approach selected at planning (e.g. nasheed-style
  vocals-only ambience vs. instrumental scoring is a **per-project planning decision**,
  stated in the production plan).
- Never present fabricated sounds as authentic religious events.

## 5. Voiceover governance

Voice style is user-controlled: 3–5 suitable profiles are presented during planning with
character, speed, tone, suitability, Bengali and (when needed) Arabic pronunciation
notes; short audition previews are generated where tooling allows; the chosen profile is
saved to `../voice-library/VOICE_SELECTION_HISTORY.md` and the project's
`VOICE_SETTINGS.md`. Full profiles: `../voice-library/VOICE_PROFILES.md`.
