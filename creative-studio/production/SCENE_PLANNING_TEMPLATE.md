## Scene doctrine reminders

- Background must match what the narration says **at that moment**.
- Text never hides the important visual information.
- Never reuse one image to fill time; vary shot scale and camera language.
- Choose the best medium per scene (3D, 2D, macro, documentary, scientific viz,
  typography-led).
