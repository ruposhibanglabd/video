# Brand Identity — Abdur Rahman Nayeem

## Creator name

**ABDUR RAHMAN NAYEEM** — displayed on finished work as an elegant English signature:
*Abdur Rahman Nayeem* (see `SIGNATURE_GUIDELINES.md`).

## Brand character

Premium · modern · cinematic · natural · elegant · educational. The brand feels like a
one-person international studio: calm authority, refined taste, respect for the subject
and the audience.

## Brand application rules

- Signature on every final video — small, subtle, consistent (never a loud watermark).
- The creator's personal photograph is used **only** for sensible creator-branding
  moments, only after it is provided and authorized — see
  `PROFILE_ASSET_GUIDELINES.md`. It is never inserted into religious or historical
  scenes inappropriately, and never replaces real visuals of the subject.
- No fictional stand-ins for the creator's appearance.

## Voice of the brand (on-screen text & narration)

Clear, warm, knowledge-respecting Bengali; precise facts; no clickbait tone; openings
that earn attention through wonder rather than shock.
