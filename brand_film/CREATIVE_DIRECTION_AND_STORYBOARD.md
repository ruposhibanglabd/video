### Bespoke Industry Demo Palettes (Scene 04)

3. **Footwear (`STRIDE`)** — Architectural Concrete `#F3F4F6`, Espresso Leather `#4A2C1D`, Carbon `#111827`
4. **Cosmetics (`LUMÉRA`)** — Rose Pearl `#FDF6F6`, Botanical Mauve `#8E4A64`, Warm Cream `#F6ECEA`
5. **Furniture (`WOODORA`)** — Sunlit Linen `#F5F1EA`, Solid Walnut `#5C3D28`, Olive Slate `#232621`
6. **Restaurant (`NOORÉ`)** — Culinary Dark Walnut `#14100E`, Saffron Amber `#E08A2E`, Warm Ivory `#FFF9F2`

### Typography & Safe Zones (9:16 Vertical `1080 × 1920`)
- **Bengali Typeface:** `Noto Sans Bengali` (Regular 400, SemiBold 600, Bold 700, ExtraBold 800) — crisp Unicode shaping rendered strictly on vector/canvas layers (zero Bengali text baked inside AI imagery).
- **Latin / Numeric Companion:** `Inter` (Regular 400, SemiBold 600, Bold 700, ExtraBold 800).
- **Editorial Layout Grid:** `76px` left/right margins (`928px` content column), `140px` top clearance, `160px` bottom clearance, generous whitespace, one clear primary Bengali message per visual beat.
- **Restraint Rules:** No heavy neon blooms, no random floating particle storms, no sci-fi HUD clutter, no distorted UI chrome.
