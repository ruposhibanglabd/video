- Champagne gold: **#BC9A55**.

Aim for premium, cinematic, modern, minimal, elegant and trustworthy. No neon, glitter, gaming graphics, exaggerated yellow gold, cheap promotional effects, mansions or luxury-car status imagery.

## Lighting and grading direction

- Rich emerald shadows, restrained champagne highlights and warm ivory light.
- Preserve the actual watch's dial, case and bracelet colors; do not recolor the product to fit the palette.
- Controlled softbox reflections, realistic brushed/polished metal texture, gentle contact shadows and believable photographic depth.
- Keep the watch and dark dial readable. Premium does not mean excessively dark.
- Balanced contrast, smooth highlight roll-off and natural skin tones in lifestyle shots.
- Use dark translucent gradients behind text where needed, not heavy flat panels across the product.
- This is an art-direction reference; no measured camera LUT or independently calibrated grading preset was created in the previous production.

## Feature-driven visual storytelling

First map the NEW product's confirmed features to distinct visuals, then generate images, record narration and edit.

**The relevant visual must appear when the corresponding feature is spoken.** Do not simply cycle attractive watch photographs under unrelated narration.

Examples from the reference campaign (use ONLY if confirmed for the new product):

- Butterfly clasp: generated open-clasp macro, restrained pointer/highlight and metallic click; do not invent mechanical operation.
- Chain-color guarantee: generated chain-finishing view and restrained shield graphic; no fabricated color-change or scratch test.
- Waterproof feature: surface water droplets on the watch and a droplet graphic; no unconfirmed depth rating, swimming, pressure jet or submersion demonstration.
- One-year warranty: accurately typeset one-year card/calendar graphic beside the product; no fabricated manufacturer certificate or extra service benefits.
- Color choices: separately generated, visually consistent product photographs, shown sequentially with short labels and controlled transitions.
- Office/formal/gifting: distinct corresponding lifestyle views and individually aligned narration; do not imply illustrative gift packaging is included.

Do not inherit previous product facts. The previous watch's BDT 1,650 price, butterfly lock, waterproof feature, chain-color guarantee and one-year warranty are NOT defaults for a new watch.

## Editing and typography

- Mobile-first 9:16, usually 35–40 seconds, 30 FPS unless the new brief says otherwise.
- Show the watch immediately; no long logo introduction.
- Separate macro hook, full-product hero, feature views, color presentation, lifestyle and price/CTA compositions.
- Use restrained camera push/pull, lateral movement, subtle light accents, match cuts/dissolves and clean typography entrances.
- Keep text concise and readable without sound. Maintain central safe areas.
- Existing logical 1080 × 1920 layout: important content roughly within x=110–970 and y=180–1690; subtitle region roughly y=1475–1640. Adapt to actual platform overlays and scale for 4K.
- Bengali: licensed Noto Sans Bengali. English: licensed Inter.
- Add Bengali text in final compositing, not inside generated photographs.
- Keep product name prominent; seller identity subordinate and explicit.
- Price must be the NEW price explicitly supplied/confirmed by the user. No invented discount, free delivery, scarcity or review claim.

## Narration and audio

- Smart, attractive, confident, warm and clearly pronounced Bangladeshi Bengali commercial delivery.
- Avoid shouting, robotic pacing and excessive speed.
- Latest reference uses the second user-selected narrator, registered as `voice-01` in the original chat. Tool voice IDs are session-scoped: do not assume this ID is registered or reusable in a fresh session. Audition/register an appropriate voice again if needed; do not promise exact voice recovery from this file alone.
- Keep one consistent speaker throughout a video.
- Record short feature/lifestyle phrases separately or split carefully at genuine sentence pauses to synchronize each visual.
- Trim only unnecessary edge silence; preserve consonants and natural pauses. Avoid aggressive tempo changes.
- Update accurate subtitles when wording changes.
- Original/licensed restrained music, soft percussion, gentle whooshes, metallic accents and final chime. Duck music below narration; avoid clipping.
- Reference loudness: approximately -15 LUFS integrated with true peak below -2 dBFS, not a mandatory fixed target for every platform.

## Export and QC

- MP4, H.264, yuv420p, AAC stereo, fast-start metadata.
- Standard social export: 1080 × 1920, 30 FPS.
- On request: vertical UHD/4K **2160 × 3840**, 30 FPS, with typography/graphics rendered at that resolution.
- Disclose when generated photographs are upscaled; do not describe them as native 4K camera footage. Avoid creating fake product detail to imply higher native resolution.
- Full-file decode, frame count/duration/resolution/codec verification, black-frame scan, clipping/loudness check and timeline visual review before delivery.
- If rendered in segments, verify exact frame continuity and avoid an unnecessary lossy re-encode when joining.
- Latest reference 4K master: 1,200 continuous frames; -14.9 LUFS integrated; -2.3 dBFS true peak.
- Facebook can recompress uploads; never guarantee that a 4K upload is displayed to every viewer in 4K. Use the original downloaded MP4, not a messaging-app-compressed copy.
